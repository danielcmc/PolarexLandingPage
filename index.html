01 The prospecting gap

A company can fit.
The timing might not.

A target account list tells you who could buy. It rarely tells you what has changed enough to make a conversation relevant today.

New engineering priorities, a shift in infrastructure, a growing team. The clues are often public, but scattered. Finding them, and understanding what they mean, takes time your team could spend selling.

Polarex connects the change to a reason to reach out.
